From a booking to a decision in four steps

Most platforms find fraud after a complaint. RideSafe checks every ride as it happens.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Ride, GPS, fare and traffic data stream in from the passenger and driver apps.

  2. 2

    Score

    The model checks routes, cancellations, trip duration and location consistency.

  3. 3

    Alert

    Staff, drivers and passengers get a push, SMS or email within seconds.

  4. 4

    Act

    Review the case, resolve it or block the account, and reroute around the jam.
